Community Guidelines

Welcome to MoodBloom. Our goal is to create a safe, respectful, and supportive space for users.

Be Respectful

Treat other users with kindness and respect. Do not post rude, hateful, threatening, or harmful comments.

Protect Your Privacy

Do not share personal information such as your address, phone number, passwords, or private details that you do not want others to see.

Use MoodBloom Responsibly

MoodBloom is meant to support mood tracking, journaling, and personal reflection. It should not be used to harass others or spread harmful content.

Be Honest and Safe

Users should be honest when using MoodBloom features and should avoid posting false, misleading, or unsafe information.

Report Problems

If you see content that seems harmful or inappropriate, please report it or contact the MoodBloom team.

Guideline Updates

These guidelines may be updated as MoodBloom continues to improve.
