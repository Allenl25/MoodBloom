Everyday Wellness

Healthy Coping Strategies

Small actions may help you pause, reset, and care for yourself during a difficult moment.

01

Slow Breathing

Breathe in slowly for four seconds, hold briefly, and breathe out slowly for four seconds.

02

Ground Yourself

Name five things you can see, four you can feel, three you can hear, two you can smell, and one you can taste.

03

Write It Down

Use your MoodBloom journal to describe what happened and how the situation made you feel.

04

Take a Short Break

Step away from the stressful situation and spend a few minutes in a quiet, comfortable place.

05

Contact Someone

Reach out to a trusted friend, family member, counselor, teacher, or another supportive person.

06

Care for Your Body

Drink water, eat something nourishing, stretch, rest, or take a short walk when possible.
